Before you start the internal Bluetooth
The console's own Bluetooth chip is shared with your gamepad. While BlueV is running, a gamepad that is switched off and on again, or reconnects, can end up connected but without input until the console is restarted.
  • Switch the gamepad on now, before pressing Start.
  • Do not switch it off or on, and do not unplug or plug its cable, while the radio is on.
  • Nothing touches the Bluetooth chip until you press Start. Turning the radio off does not undo the problem.
